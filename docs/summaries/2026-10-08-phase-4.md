# Phase 4 summary — The wallet cannot overspend

Window: 2026-10-08 · Baseline: Phase 3 summary (`docs/summaries/2026-10-08-phase-3.md`)

## 1. Headline

**This round: 4 tasks, 4 done. Payments are governed by a config-file policy
with per-request and per-day ceilings, every decision lands in a JSONL
ledger, and the footer shows the armed wallet and today's spend.**

Routine: `X-Cost` response headers are captured as ledger observations;
day accounting runs on UTC days, recorded on each entry.

## 2. What it means

An autonomous wallet now has a hard budget: nothing signs above the
per-request ceiling, and the day total stops payments at the daily cap, with
the refusal reason recorded either way. The ledger (`​.pi/x402-ledger.jsonl`)
is the audit trail the plan promised, and its writer-failure isolation means
a broken disk never breaks inference. The footer follows the existing
preferred-provider pattern, so interactive users see spend state without any
new surface to learn.

## 3. Still open

- The live paid request remains unproven (no funded wallet on this machine);
  policy and ledger are stub-proven.
- The ledger is per-project append-only; nothing rolls or archives it.
- Refusals notify via the ledger only; a one-shot UI notify per reason was
  scoped in the brief but landed as ledger records — the footer reflects
  state, not events.
- Composition with third-party hooks is contractual but only tested against
  this repo's own stubs; Phase 5 hardens it.
