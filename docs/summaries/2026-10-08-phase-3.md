## 2. What it means

Every fetch the extension makes — streams via `options.fetch`, direct calls
via the global wrap, preferred-provider routes through the auth rewrite — now
flows through one payment loop. The loop still refuses everything except
valid, allowlisted, under-ceiling challenges, so with no wallet configured the
behavior is bit-identical to before. The composition contract is the part
other extensions will rely on: a non-enumerable `Symbol.for` marker exposes
the inner fetch and the live config, and re-wrapping reconfigures in place.

## 3. Still open

- The live Surp session proof remains blocked on a funded wallet, unchanged
  since Phase 1; everything paid is stub-proven only.
- Ceilings are hard-coded (0.25 USDC per request) and the allowlist is the
  probe default; the real config surface is Phase 4.
- Refusals are silent returns, not logged errors; the ledger is Phase 4.
- The footer shows nothing about wallet or spend; Phase 4 again.
